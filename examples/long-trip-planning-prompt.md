# Long-trip planning example

Use `$packmap-travel-packing` to plan this trip from candidate list through pre-packing map.

I am one traveler taking a six-month study and leisure trip from East Asia to Europe. The trip has four stages:

1. A four-week summer language course in a warm city with weekday classes and weekend day trips.
2. A six-week rail-travel period using one city as a base while the large suitcases stay at the accommodation.
3. A three-month university exchange in a cool, rainy city, including orientation and one formal event.
4. A one-week cold and windy outdoor trip before returning home.

I can do laundry weekly. I will take two 28-inch checked suitcases and one 25-liter backpack. I prefer comfortable student-casual clothes, want one photo-ready outfit, will hike occasionally, use skincare and makeup, and want to leave at least 20% luggage space for purchases.

Please:

- generate a complete but editable candidate list;
- mark bring, buy locally, optional, and skip decisions;
- calculate clothing quantities by the laundry cycle;
- create airport, first-night, daily-access, warm-weather, cold-weather, and formal-event modules;
- split delay-critical basics across the two checked suitcases;
- mark carry-on and checked-only items;
- assign every selected item to a suitcase, compartment, or pouch;
- include missing-item, liquid, battery, valuable-item, and weight-buffer warnings;
- output PackMap JSON and organizer-compatible TXT.
