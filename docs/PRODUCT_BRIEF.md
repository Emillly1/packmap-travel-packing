# Product Brief

## Product

PackMap is a travel packing planner that converts a trip into a searchable location map across luggage, compartments, pouches, and individual items.

## Problem

Flat checklists answer "what should I bring?" but not:

- how much is realistic for the laundry cycle;
- what must be carried on or checked;
- what should stay accessible during transit or the first night;
- how to split essentials across multiple bags;
- where an item was actually packed.

Complex travelers therefore maintain several disconnected notes and still repack repeatedly.

## Primary users

1. Study-abroad, exchange, and relocation travelers.
2. Multi-city or multi-season travelers with checked luggage.
3. People who want a precise packing record and departure audit.

Secondary users include weekend travelers, business travelers, families, and outdoor travelers.

## Value proposition

PackMap creates a useful free plan without AI, then turns that plan into a location-aware packing workspace. Future AI improves personalization but does not gate the core workflow.

## Product principles

- Planning should end in an executable map.
- Explicit user preferences override template defaults.
- Recommendations must remain editable and explainable.
- Safety-sensitive rules are warnings, not universal clearance.
- The first-night and airport experience are first-class packing stages.
- User data remains exportable and portable.

## Success criteria for MVP

- A new user completes a first map without external instructions.
- At least 70% of beta users reach the generated packing map.
- Median setup time is below 8 minutes for a normal trip.
- Users can locate a packed item in under 10 seconds.
- JSON export and restore are lossless.
- No critical carry-on/checked violation is silently accepted.

## Non-goals for MVP

- Accounts, payments, live collaboration, social feeds, marketplace templates.
- Medical prescribing, immigration advice, or airline clearance.
- Accurate physical volume simulation from product dimensions.
- AI-generated recommendations or external transmission of trip data.
