# Usage Terms

PackMap is a planning and organization tool. Its recommendations and warnings are informational and may be incomplete.

- Airline, airport, rail, ferry, customs, immigration, and restricted-item rules must be confirmed with the relevant official source.
- Medical and medication content does not replace advice from a qualified clinician or pharmacist.
- Users remain responsible for travel documents, legal compliance, insurance, backups, and final packing decisions.
- Beta behavior and file formats may evolve, but PackMap will not intentionally overwrite a document that fails validation.

The `2.0.0` JSON export is the preferred recovery format during Beta.
